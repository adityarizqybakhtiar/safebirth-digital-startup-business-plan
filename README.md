# SafeBirth – Digital Startup Business Plan

This repository contains a portfolio preview of my final thesis project, **SafeBirth**, a non-emergency maternal health companion startup application. The project was developed as part of the undergraduate thesis in the Information Systems program at Universitas Mikroskil.

## Project Overview

SafeBirth is designed as a non-emergency maternal health companion application that helps pregnant women, prospective mothers, and partners access clearer guidance, maternal education, early consultation, and healthcare facility information in one structured digital platform.

The application focuses on supporting users in understanding pregnancy-related complaints, accessing non-emergency consultation, receiving maternal education, and finding relevant health professionals or healthcare facilities based on their needs.

## Problem Background

Maternal health still faces challenges related to limited access to clear and reliable initial guidance, especially for pregnant women in understanding physical and psychological conditions during pregnancy. Many pregnant women may experience confusion in distinguishing normal pregnancy complaints from danger signs, as well as stress, anxiety, and concerns about fetal conditions.

SafeBirth was proposed as a digital solution to provide structured, informative, and non-emergency maternal assistance.

## Main Features

SafeBirth includes several core features:

- Initial pregnancy complaint assistance
- Non-emergency consultation with health professionals or maternal counselors
- Maternal mental health support
- Location-based healthcare facility search
- Maternal and fetal health education
- Nutrition guidance
- Pregnancy and fertility calendar
- Prenatal and postnatal classes
- Mother community forum

## Product Scope

SafeBirth is positioned as a **non-emergency maternal companion platform**. It does not provide emergency medical services, medical diagnosis, or direct treatment. Instead, it provides early guidance, educational support, and direction to healthcare facilities when needed.

## Revenue Model

SafeBirth uses a combination of three revenue streams:

- **Product-driven revenue**: subscription packages, bundling, and consultation services
- **Partner-driven revenue**: healthcare partner profiles, partner campaigns, and event/class commissions
- **Sales-driven revenue**: collaboration with hospitals, clinics, government programs, and CSR initiatives

## Financial Projection Summary

The financial projection shows that SafeBirth is expected to experience losses in the first and second years due to launching, user acquisition, service development, and operational strengthening. However, the business is projected to generate a positive net profit in the third year if user growth, transactions, partnerships, and institutional collaboration meet the base-case scenario.
